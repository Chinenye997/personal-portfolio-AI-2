Ntachi-Osa Restaurant Customer Sentiment Analysis

A sentiment analysis project based on customer discussions from TikTok and YouTube to understand public opinion about Ntachi-Osa Restaurant.

Data Source

TikTok & YouTube

Comments Analyzed

137 Customer Comments

Key Findings

Analysis of 137 customer comments revealed that the restaurant's food quality and pricing received consistent praise. However, customer service emerged as the biggest challenge, with a recurring complaint that women were often served after men, particularly at the New Heaven branch.

Sentiment Breakdown

😊 Positive 20%
😐 Neutral 48%
😞 Negative 31%

Conclusion

Overall, the analysis shows that customers genuinely appreciate Ntachi-Osa Restaurant's food quality and pricing. The primary opportunity for improvement lies in customer service consistency, particularly at the New Heaven branch.
